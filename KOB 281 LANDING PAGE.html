About Us

Thusanang Communication Consulting is an organisational communication advisory firm that helps organisations improve leadership communication, team collaboration, and employee engagement. We support organisations facing communication challenges during mergers, restructuring, and organisational change.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To help organisations build effective communication systems that strengthen collaboration, improve leadership interaction, and support sustainable organisational performance.

Our Vision

To become a trusted communication consulting partner that empowers organisations to transform communication challenges into opportunities for growth.

Our Services

Communication Diagnostics

We analyse communication breakdowns and identify their root causes.

Leadership Communication Training

We help leaders communicate clearly and manage workplace conflict effectively.

Cross-Cultural Communication Consulting

We support teams from different cultures to communicate and collaborate better.

Organisational Change Communication

We help organisations communicate mergers, restructuring, and change more effectively.
